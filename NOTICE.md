The first public version intentionally starts from a traceable Markdown snapshot instead of scraped website HTML. Article pages link back to their exact upstream baseline files.

## First-person material

Some inherited lessons contain first-person anecdotes written by the upstream author or contributors. Until an article is explicitly rewritten and sourced by AiChengGong, those anecdotes must not be represented as personal experiences of “不懂AI的陈工”. The public site displays this distinction on every article page.
